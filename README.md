FasstPS AlbumKit — Beta 1
Professional Photo Album Design Software for Photographers, Printing Labs & Album Designers
Welcome to FasstPS AlbumKit Beta 1, developed and supported by Vigsai Pvt. Ltd.

FasstPS AlbumKit is designed for photographers, photography studios, printing labs, and professional album designers who work with digital photographs and Photoshop-based PSD album templates.

This beta release invites users to test the software, explore its workflow, and share feedback to help improve future releases.

⚠️ System Requirement — Adobe Photoshop Version 8
Adobe Photoshop Version 8 is required.

Before installing and using FasstPS AlbumKit, ensure that the required Photoshop version is installed on your Windows PC.

Compatibility with other Photoshop versions has not been confirmed for this beta release.

Please verify that your system meets the software's requirements before beginning your album design work.

FasstPS AlbumKit is designed specifically for the professional photo album industry.

Photographers: Prepare photographs and manage projects for wedding, pre-wedding, and other photo albums.
Photography Studios: Organize album projects and streamline their design workflow.
Printing Labs: Prepare album designs and supported output files for printing workflows.
Professional Album Designers: Work with PSD templates, arrange album pages, and prepare designs for further editing.
Photo Album Businesses: Evaluate the software for their existing album production workflow.
